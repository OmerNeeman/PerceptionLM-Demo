# Brief — S6: index all 8 scenes, and let the app reach them

**Dispatch:** subagent. **Proves:** **F-1 at scale**, **F-7**, **N-1 at scale**,
**N-2**. **Depends on:** S5 (green).

## Goal

Index the remaining **7 scenes / 97,433 tiles**, and make the local app serve
**all** indexed AOIs with a working **date filter**. Indexing scenes the app
cannot reach is not worth doing, so both halves are this stage.

## Why this matters to the owner

Right now the demo can only be asked about a tent camp. **Half the vocabulary
the owner named has nothing to find** — rubble, debris, collapsed roofs live in
`leb` and `gaza`. And `leb` is a **destruction pair**: intact village core in
2022-10-29, the same pixels bright rubble in 2025-06-06. That is the most
compelling content in the corpus and it is currently unreachable.

## Part 2 — F-7, the date filter

Read F-7's amendment block. Summary: implemented and tested against **`leb`
only**, because it is the sole true multi-date pair; the *mechanism* is general
so other AOIs can be dated later without code change.

- Scenes with no known date carry `date: unknown` and are **excluded** from
  date-filtered queries — never silently included.
- *Expected:* filtering `leb` to `2025-06-06` halves that AOI's candidate pool
  and every returned id carries that date; an undated AOI returns nothing under
  a date filter and **does not raise**.

## Part 3 — the app serves all AOIs

- An **AOI selector**, defaulting to one AOI rather than everything, so results
  stay interpretable. Include an explicit "all AOIs" option.
- The **date control becomes live for `leb`** and stays honestly disabled
  elsewhere — it currently reads "No dated imagery indexed for this AOI", which
  is the right behaviour; keep that for undated scenes.
- **U-6:** active AOI and date filters visible with the result count they
  produced, clearable in one action.
- Cross-AOI ranking within a scale is legitimate — one embedder, one space — but
  note in the UI that a 448 px tile is **46.91 m in `leb` and 44.80 m in the UTM
  scenes**, a 4.7% difference. Get extents from `geo.py`; never hardcode.
- **Two CRSs now coexist** (EPSG:3857 for `leb`, EPSG:32636 for the rest). Only
  the geo-lookup layer should care. Do not let a CRS assumption leak into
  ranking or filtering.

## Part 4 — re-measure N-1 at real scale

Top-10 over the **full** index in **< 200 ms on CPU**. At 9,631 tiles it was
9-18 ms; at 108,542 it will land far closer to the budget. **Measure and paste.**
numpy exact brute force — no faiss, no sklearn wrapper, no Python loop.

Also report **process RSS** with the full index loaded (108,542 x 768 fp16 is
~167 MB of vectors before overhead), and the corpus load time against F-5's 5 s.

**If N-1 exceeds 200 ms, stop and flag it.** Do not silently add an
approximate index — `spec.md` §7 excludes ANN by explicit decision.
